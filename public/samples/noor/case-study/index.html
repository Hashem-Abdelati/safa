A restaurant website with atmosphere—and a clear way to book.

Restaurant website concept2026English + العربية
01 / Challenge

Restaurant websites often ask customers to work too hard.

Menus hide in PDFs, booking pathways compete with clutter, and the atmosphere gets lost behind a template. The task was to make the essentials immediate without flattening the personality of the room.

02 / Direction

Quiet confidence, designed into every pathway.

Large editorial photography sets the mood. Warm neutrals, a sharp serif, thin rules, and generous spacing keep the experience calm. Menu, reservation, location, and hours are never more than a tap away.
